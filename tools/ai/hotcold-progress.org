* S1 Source
** S1.1 The compiler places blocks that are cold by construction (decline/slow paths, signal/error exits, the out-of-line String branch of guarded slot reads, GC-poll slow paths, overflow fallbacks) after the hot code of the function, for at least wf_bytecode_w_fast_run and the functions with an alignhot entry, with branch sense chosen so the hot path falls through; semantics unchanged (IR-level and emitted-code tests, red/green)
#+NOTE: Cold arms: loop-guard decline stores (effect if, either arm) and the nl_string_read64 call arm of value-position guarded reads; they are queued per defun and emitted after the epilogue ret and the internal entry. 81 arms in the host compile of fast_run. Red = seed compiler (5 of 8 fail), green = 8 tests, negative control = flipped branch polarity makes the e2e tests fail. Signal exits, GC-poll slow paths and overflow fallbacks of fast_run are not separate IR shapes here (they are the decline stores or calls to other units), so only the two shapes above are moved. Other alignhot-entry functions (execute_frame, w_function, ...) were NOT measured for deferred arms: the site gate needs a guarded while loop (declines) or a nl_string_read64 call, and prefix-cache facts absent, so many of them may defer nothing; the criterion is met on fast_run only.
