#+TITLE: Source interpreter per-form cost, GNU eval_sub structure (interp4)
#+NOTE: Created 2026-11-02 before implementation. Seed = coordinator HEAD 471535ddb (v19) + overlay (vmcore, corecall). Baselines: v19 integ-1036 (per-form audit, first pass), v20 integ-1037 (= v19 + vmcore + corecall; all before/after numbers are against v20 once its callgrind table is in target/interp4/ir-base.json).
#+NOTE: S1 cause (PMC, r5-sum-base-toplevel, 300000 trips, CPU 13): NeLisp 4590 Ir / 1847 cycles per trip (IPC 2.48), GNU 2247 Ir / 609 cycles (IPC 3.69); store-to-load forwards 917 vs 64 per trip; mispredicts negligible on both. Each call layer of the evaluator spills its arguments and locals to a 0x100-0x560 byte frame, so removing layers lowers Ir and cycles; per-layer trimming alone cannot reach GNU wall time.
#+NOTE: Per-form targets (<= 2x GNU Ir, v19 first-pass numbers, extra Ir over the empty loop): (< i n) 1471 -> <= 1206 (GNU 603); (aref a 1) 1014 -> <= 808 (GNU 404); (car (quote ..)) 1262 -> <= 732 (GNU 366; 179 of it is the quote arm); (setq i (+ i 1)) 1431 already inside 2x of 792 (target <= 1584, not claimed); (let ((y 1)) y) 5214 -> <= 2146 (GNU 1073).
#+NOTE: survey4 row rule: interp rows of survey4 whose key functions are native subrs only (resurvey19 survey4.jsonl, family 1), minus rows whose cost is a slow subr body (cs-fmt-*, cs-str2num-*, cs-num2str-*) and minus the closure-call rows (mp-fib-20, mp-tak: call protocol). Median over exactly the remaining rows before and after.
#+NOTE: Scope cut, 2026-11-02, before the first build: the planned "fold the evaluator boundary bookkeeping into nl_eval_inner_w" (old S2.5) and "fuse nl_eic_subr_call_w with nl_eic_prim_frame_w" were not built: the first needs the same body spliced into the shared shim entry `nelisp_eval_call_w` (every evaluation in the runtime) and could not be verified on the host harness, with two builds available. The denominator fell from 12 to 11 criteria, then S4.3 (the 2x targets, red) was added: 12. The per-form targets stay the measure; forms that miss them are reported as misses.
