#+TITLE: NeLisp shallow binding for special variables
#+NOTE: Objective (user, 2026-10-18): replace deep binding (every dynamic variable
#+NOTE: read walks the whole frame stack, comparing symbols by name) with GNU Emacs
#+NOTE: style shallow binding: the global mirror entry's value slot holds the
#+NOTE: current dynamic value, a binding saves the old value in its frame, and
#+NOTE: unbinding restores it.  Lexical variables keep the frame cells.
#+NOTE: Plan: S1 converts binders and unbinders only (readers that walk frames
#+NOTE: then find nothing and fall back to the mirror, so answers stay right);
#+NOTE: S2 short-circuits dynamic reads and writes to the mirror.  Per-thread
#+NOTE: bindings (an SBCL-style thread-local slot per symbol) are a Doc 214
#+NOTE: merge requirement, recorded in S3, not built on this branch.
#+NOTE: Baseline (fd786569d, tools/ai/shallow-binding-baseline.txt): regexp
#+NOTE: 6,099 us/match, dynread 2.44 us, interpreter-only dynread 6.23 us and
#+NOTE: 13.06 us under 40 nested bindings.  GNU 31.1: 0.5 us, 0.10 us, 0.09 us.
#+NOTE: Shallow binding removes the walk and its allocations (~15k of 37k per
#+NOTE: regexp match); it is not expected to reach GNU on the regexp alone --
#+NOTE: byte-code value boxing and collection cost remain.
