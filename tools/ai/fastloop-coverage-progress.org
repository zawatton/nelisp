#+NOTE: Deliberately deferred. Bcall polls at an instruction boundary with operands/code/constants rooted in the full frame/footer; its direct closure path initializes every callee slot and publishes the root top before allocating, and its fallback wf_bytecode_call owns argument rooting. On return it can replace MEM/SP/code/constants and invalidates last-name. Binding opcodes additionally coordinate AUX, BINDINGS, unwind records and error state in execute_frame, outside the owned starter masks. Copying only the call's final PC/SP updates is insufficient. No cons/list, varbind/unbind or varset fast leaf is added; extending that publication/unwind protocol is not paid for in this lane.
