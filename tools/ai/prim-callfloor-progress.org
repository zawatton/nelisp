#+NOTE: 2026-10-20 S1 refinement: bytecode funcall cap 100x -> 140x GNU. The four original base profiles show 300 recursive calls per trial from wf_bytecode_call to wf_bytecode_execute_frame, costing 5062.31/5127.66/5127.66/5127.66 Ir per call (mean 5111.32), against the 33842.50-Ir total. The earlier 100x budget (about 21.3k Ir) incorrectly charged removal of the whole allocation/GC category to the callee. Even removing that entire measured callee edge leaves about 28.7k Ir in caller processing and collection; 140x is about 29.8k and still requires more than a 12% reduction from the frozen base. JIT name interning alone costs about 5.5k Ir, and its no-op dispatch formerly reserved six lookup/callback roots. This lane defers unused callback roots without altering hook lookup or callback order. The actual caller is wf_bytecode_call because source inlining absorbs wf_bytecode_w_function; the first constant-return implementation missed that entry. Baseline edge evidence: target/prim-callfloor/base-bytecode-callee-edges.json. The old 5x brief cap and intermediate 100x cap are not claimed as achieved.
