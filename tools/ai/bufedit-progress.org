* S2 Fix with red/green checks and a GNU-parity corpus

** S2.1 Design recorded in the prelude: u32-per-character gap store, gap geometry, the multibyte choice and its cost
#+NOTE: store slot = unibyte string of 4*CAP bytes; BLEN is the gap start, LEN the count; point = PEND or BLEN+1; unibyte slot; INS slot is now the "has wide text" flag so slices of a multibyte buffer are multibyte strings (GNU make_buffer_string). Cost: 4 bytes per character (a 1 MB buffer holds 4 MB), a UTF-8 encode pass in buffer-substring/buffer-string (about 6 instructions per character, 2.5 for ASCII) and a decode pass in insert, in exchange for O(1) positions, one-load reads and memmove-speed gap moves with no char<->byte map.
