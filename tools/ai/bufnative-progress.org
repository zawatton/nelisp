#+NOTE: line functions: nelisp--motion-bol walks back with interpreted aref (about 590 us for a 500-char line, flat over buffer size); needs a native backward newline search over the two gap strings (GNU find_newline/memrchr) plus bodies for bolp, eolp, line-beginning-position, line-end-position, pos-bol, pos-eol, forward-line, count-lines (str-count-nl exists). forward-line is O(buffer) over nelisp-buffer-string for N>1 (80/245/1858 us at 1k/10k/100k).
#+NOTE: gap buffer (GNU buffer.h, insdel.c): one byte region BEG_ADDR..GPT_ADDR | gap | GAP_END..Z_ADDR with gpt, gap_size, z, begv, zv, pt in chars and bytes; make_gap growth, move_gap_both memmove on a far edit, UTF-8 text with a char/byte position cache (buf_charpos_to_bytepos); markers carry charpos and bytepos. 105 sites read before-gap/after-gap directly (69 prelude, 6 lisp/nelisp-stdlib-misc.el, 30 src/nelisp-buffer.el: rx2 regexp windows, line walkers, textprop/overlay adjusters), so first make the read API (nelisp-buffer-substring, char-at, search window) the only access, then swap the representation inside settle/insert/delete-region. Removes: front/mid insert O(distance), buffer-string after an edit O(buffer), forward-line O(buffer).
